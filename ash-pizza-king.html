The Fire

One oven.
One temperature.
No shortcuts.

Our oven is fed oak and cherry wood, hand-split every morning and burned down to embers before the first dough hits the peel. The heat never drops below 850°F — hot enough to blister the crust before the toppings even think about overcooking.

It's the same method Marco learned in a stone-floored kitchen outside Naples: flour, water, salt, time — and a fire that does the rest.
